[CLINICAL PROBLEM // CORE PARADIGM]

Unified Clinical Intake vs. Fragmented Screening

Eliminating multi-form data fragmentation with an integrated predictive intelligence framework for concurrent metabolic and cardiovascular evaluation.

Unified Intake, Dual Model Inference

Traditional clinical risk assessment is hindered by disconnected specialty workflows: clinicians collect repetitive questionnaires for diabetes while cardiovascular screenings require separate, redundant entries.

I-HEART establishes a single comprehensive intake record spanning demographics, anthropometrics, hemodynamics, laboratory biomarkers, and lifestyle habits. From this single submission, backend mappers extract specialized feature vectors to run independent machine-learning models simultaneously:

  • Diabetes Risk Pipeline: Evaluates glycemic markers (fasting blood glucose, HbA1c), body mass index, age, and metabolic indicators.
  • Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) Pipeline: Evaluates systolic/diastolic blood pressure, lipid fractions (total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides), vascular history, and lifestyle factors.

Concurrent Risk Synthesis & XAI Attribution

Predictions and calibrated probability scores are synthesized into a unified diagnostic dashboard. Rather than presenting opaque probabilities, I-HEART attributes elevated risk to specific clinical factors exceeding physiological reference ranges.

Clinical Screening Objective: Models are trained with balanced class weighting to maximize Recall (Sensitivity), ensuring early detection of at-risk patients while minimizing dangerous false negatives.

Academic Background & Clinical Motivation

Non-communicable diseases such as Type 2 Diabetes and Cardiovascular Disease share interconnected pathophysiological pathways — including insulin resistance, chronic hypertension, and dyslipidemia. However, contemporary screening tools routinely evaluate these conditions in isolation.

I-HEART demonstrates how an integrated patient representation paired with dedicated ML models provides healthcare practitioners with unified, comprehensive risk visibility in a single screening workflow.

Target Domain 1: Type 2 Diabetes
Early identification of glycemic vulnerability based on fasting glucose, HbA1c, body composition, and metabolic history.
Target Domain 2: Cardiovascular Disease
Estimation of vascular risk utilizing hemodynamic metrics, blood pressure, lipid profiles, and lifestyle indicators.
Hospital / EMR Integration Foundation (`/api/emr/analyze`):

The platform includes an architectural adapter layer capable of receiving EHR/EMR payloads, normalizing external clinical codes, and routing patient profiles into the identical predictive pipelines without requiring manual clinician data entry.
