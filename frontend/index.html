Architecture Concept

Project Overview

A unified predictive framework built to streamline multi-disease risk evaluation in modern healthcare workflows.

Unified Input, Dual Target Assessment

Traditional risk prediction tools require healthcare providers or individuals to fill out separate, redundant questionnaires for every condition. This system is architected around one unified patient health profile containing demographics, vital signs, physical measurements, laboratory indicators, and lifestyle factors.

The unified profile is mapped and routed into two dedicated machine-learning pipelines:

  • Diabetes Risk Pipeline: Analyzes glucose levels, BMI, insulin factors, age, and metabolic indicators.
  • Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) Pipeline: Analyzes blood pressure, cholesterol ratios, vascular history, and lifestyle factors.

Unified Dashboard Presentation

Predictions and risk scores are consolidated into a single unified results dashboard, giving clinicians and patients a holistic view of metabolic and cardiovascular risks simultaneously.

Academic Milestone Note: The current 30% prototype uses deterministic clinical heuristics to demonstrate the complete end-to-end data pipeline. Real ML models will be trained on medical datasets in subsequent phases.

Project Scope

About the Project

An academic research endeavor targeting early non-communicable disease risk identification through artificial intelligence.

Academic Background & Motivation

This is a final-year academic engineering project titled "AI-Based Explainable Health Risk Prediction System for Diabetes and Cardiovascular Disease". Non-communicable conditions like diabetes and cardiovascular disease share interconnected metabolic risk factors but are frequently evaluated in isolation.

Our research demonstrates how a unified patient representation combined with specialized machine-learning architectures and SHAP explainability can streamline early risk detection.

Target Area 1: Diabetes
Early identification of type-2 diabetes risk based on glycemic indicators, body composition, and lifestyle predictors.
Target Area 2: Cardiovascular Disease
Risk estimation for cardiovascular conditions utilizing hemodynamic metrics, lipid profiles, and patient history.
Future Hospital / EHR Database Integration Architecture:

In the future, authorized hospital/EHR systems can provide patient information to the same unified data-processing pipeline via Patient ID. Manual data entry is used for the current prototype.
